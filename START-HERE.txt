This build uses a booking-request flow. Customers do not pay on the website.

1. Open config.js and verify business details, prices, service areas and Firebase settings.
2. In Firebase, enable Firestore and Email/Password Authentication.
3. Create an admin account and an admins/{uid} document with active: true.
4. Deploy firestore.rules and firestore.indexes.json.
5. In the admin dashboard, publish availability separately for each operating region.
6. Optionally deploy the Firestore-triggered email function after setting SMTP_USER and SMTP_PASS.
7. Enable Firebase App Check before public launch.
8. Replace images and remove any claims the business cannot evidence.

Customer promise: a request is acknowledged immediately; availability, final price and payment instructions are confirmed within one working day.
